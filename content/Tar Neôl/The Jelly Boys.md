## 🫧 Undercity Information Syndicate

### 🧠 **Core Concept**

A **secretive guild of spies, blackmailers, and extortionists**, the Jelly Boys don’t break your bones — they break your life. They operate like an oozing network of slime-coated informants, listening in cracks, dripping through society’s seams, and dissolving reputations in acid when needed.

---

### 🫃 **Leader: “The Gelly”**
[Gelly](Hidden/NPC's/Gelly.md)
A **gelatinous cube crime lord** with:

- **Transparent, shifting body** with floating scrolls, faces, and tokens of debt suspended inside.  
- A central **mental core** (possibly a brain in a jar or embedded skull) that **emits psychic speech**.
- Speaks via **telepathy**, voice like oozing ink and cold stone.
- Rumored to **consume secrets**, literally digesting blackmail material to retain it permanently.
- Rarely seen. Operates from a **viscous throne chamber**.

> **Quote:**  
> _“You forget... but I digest. All things rot in time — especially trust.”_

---

### 📍 **Headquarters Location: The Oozehouse**

**Location Name**: _The Oozehouse_  
**Location Type**: Sunken noble estate turned slime-ridden spy den  
**Setting**:

- Located in a **collapsed gallery hall** beneath a drowned part of the undercity called **Marrowwash Reach**.
- Accessible through **a fake shrine**, a **sewer filtration shaft**, or **a moss-covered elevator ruin** operated by whisper-password.
- Walls are **slimy, pulsing**, and warded with **anti-divination magic**.
- Decor: Melted paintings, jars of preserved tongues, whispering pipes.

---

### 🕶️ **Structure of the Jelly Boys**
- **Whispers**: Entry-level pickpockets and street spies.
- **Thinners**: Interrogators, rumor-brokers, blackmailers.
- **Disgels**: Disposers, cleaners, and disappearers.
- **Jell-Born**: Closest to the Gelly — altered with slimy grafts, fluid movements, and slippery charm.

---

### 📡 **Business Practices**

- Trade in:    
    - **Secrets** from nobles, politicians, mages
    - **Blackmail documents** and **memory thefts**
    - Selling **safe passage or silence**
- Known to **forgive debts in exchange for favors**, often ones you don’t understand until it’s too late.
