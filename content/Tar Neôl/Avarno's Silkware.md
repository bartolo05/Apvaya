## 🪡 Avarno’s Silkware

**Type:** Luxury Textile Boutique  
**Location:** Merchant Quarter, Tar Neôl  
**Owner:** Avarno de’Saeth (Tiefling crime-lord / fashion mogul)  
**Public Reputation:** Prestigious, eccentric, favored by nobles and wealthy merchants

---

### 🏛️ Shop Description

- Gilded copper sign shaped like a flowing bolt of silk hangs above the door.
- Interior draped in vivid colors:
    - Deep emeralds
    - Rich crimsons
    - Iridescent blues shimmering like beetle wings
- The air smells of:
    - Fine incense
    - Dyed silks
    - Expensive perfumes
- Shelves display:
    - Coils of rare fabrics
    - Embroidered cloaks
    - Exotic accessories like **feathered fans, jeweled veils, and silver-thread gloves**
- Crystal lanterns provide soft lighting that makes silk shimmer magically.

---

### 🎭 Assistant on Duty

**Name:** Pelza Marr  
**Race:** Male Half-Elf  
**Appearance:**

- Lean, delicate features    
- Impeccably dressed in a high-collared silk tunic of deep bronze
- Short, neatly trimmed hair
- Wears elegant rings on nearly every finger

**Personality:**

- Extremely polite but faintly condescending
- Speaks with precise diction
- Admires fashion above all else
- Slightly flustered that Avarno is absent

---

### 🗣️ Pelza’s Dialogue Snippets

> _“Welcome, gentlefolk, to Avarno’s Silkware… alas, the master is indisposed today.”_
> _“I assure you, anything you choose will transform you into the very envy of Tar Neôl.”_
> _“Perhaps I could interest you in a bespoke ensemble? Avarno himself sketched the design.”_
> _“Information? Hmph… we sell silk, not secrets… though silk sometimes carries whispers, yes?”_

---

### 🪡 Possible Interactions

- Party browses for:    
    - Disguises
    - Nobility-worthy clothing
    - Hidden pockets sewn into garments
- Pelza might:
    - Drop hints about Avarno’s connections if sufficiently charmed or bribed.
    - Be protective of Avarno’s privacy.
- Shop doubles as:
    - A front for Avarno’s up-and-coming crime faction
    - A subtle contact point for clandestine deals

---

### 🎲 Secrets Hidden in the Shop

- Secret backroom with:    
    - Ledgers of wealthy clients (some potentially blackmail material)
    - Special silks laced with magical fibers
- Concealed compartments in display tables used for passing secret messages.

---

### 🎭 Plot Hooks

- Pelza might recognize the party from rumors about recent undercity dealings.    
- A message for Avarno might arrive while the party is present, containing:
    - Names
    - Locations
    - Coded instructions tied to the Crystal Lantern
- Another customer could be a noble linked to Avarno’s crime operations.
