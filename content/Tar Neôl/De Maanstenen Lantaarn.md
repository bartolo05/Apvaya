### 🪨 **Description**

Deep beneath Tar Neôl, nestled in the **cradle of a petrified colossus**, lies _The Moonstone Lantern_. Carved between the **stone roots of a long-dead titan tree**, the tavern’s gnarled walls glimmer faintly with embedded **moonstones**. These eerie gems cast a **soft, silvery light**, creating pockets of gloom and clarity throughout the cavernous den.

No one remembers who first built the tavern — some say it was grown, not carved — but for generations, it has served as a **neutral ground for underworld dealings**.

---

### 🔮 **Notable Features**

- **Fossilized Root Walls**: The bar, booths, and floor are uneven and vein-like, shaped around the natural root formation.
- **Moonstone Light**: Subtle, shifting glows from the stones dimly illuminate patrons' faces — perfect for shady deals.
- **No Windows**: Only one entrance through a **narrow tunnel** accessed via the **abandoned vent-shaft market** in the undercity.
