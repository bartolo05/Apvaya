## 🛠️ The Brazen Gritpour

**Type:** Copper Foundry / Criminal Front  
**Location:** Industrial Quarter, Tar Neôl  
**Associated Faction:** Hollow Coin Syndicate  
**Public Function:**

- Refining copper ore into ingots and sheets
- Producing copper rooftiles and architectural copper
- Important industrial hub of Tar Neôl

---

### 🏭 Description

- Smokestacks belch faint greenish smoke from copper impurities.   
- Exterior walls streaked with verdigris (green patina).
- Interior lit by orange and green flickers of molten copper.
- Foundry floor crowded with crucibles, rolling presses, and hammering stations.
- Smell of hot metal, oil, and burning slag.
---

### 🔥 Secret Operations

- Front for the Hollow Coin Syndicate.
- Guards disguised as copper workers monitor the floor.
- Hidden vaults for:
    - Voidglass shards
    - Black-market weapon shipments
    - Gang funds
- Roars from Karzal’s Pit sometimes rumble faintly beneath the foundry floor.
---

## 🔑 Entrance to Karzal’s Pit

### **The Furnace Gate**

- Hidden entrance concealed inside a disused copper furnace on the west wall.
- Access granted only to those with a **bitten copper coin**.
- Entry process:
    - Guard inspects coin.
    - Taps it on furnace wall in specific rhythm.
    - Interior rotates, revealing stone stairway descending underground.
- Security Measures:
    - Scalding steam trap triggers if forced.
    - Arcane lock prevents unauthorized use.
---

### 🪙 Bitten Coin

- Copper coin with a chunk bitten out of one edge.
- Symbol of Hollow Coin membership or special access.
- Used as ticket for:
    - Arena entry
    - Secret meetings
    - Negotiations with Karzal
---

## 🎭 Crowd Management
- Patrons disguise themselves as workers during fight nights.
- Three classes of entry:
    - **Slag Tunnel (low-class access)**
    - **Verdigris Freight Lift (public mid-tier)**
    - **Smelter Catwalks (VIP access)**
- Hollow Coin guards maintain order and keep prying eyes away.
---

## 🌆 Atmosphere
- Heatwaves shimmer in the air.
- Clangs and hammering echo like war drums.
- Sparks shower down from catwalks.
- Green and orange light reflects off sweating workers and copper surfaces.
- Subtle vibration from fights in Karzal’s Pit below.

---

### Potential Plot Hooks
- Party needs to return **Cindervow** to Karzal.  
- Ghaldran might be plotting sabotage of the foundry.
- Hollow Coin may try to recruit or test the party in the pit.
- Foundry workers could provide secret intel for the right price.
