## 🪙 Karzal’s Pit & Hollow Coin Base

**Location:** Beneath The Brazen Gritpour, Tar Neôl  
**Faction:** Hollow Coin Syndicate  
**Purpose:** Gladiatorial arena, criminal HQ, safehouse

---

### 🪙 Arena Design: The Bitten Coin

- Arena floor shaped like a **perfect copper coin**—but with a large **crescent-shaped recess “bitten” out of one side.**
- Karzal’s **stone throne** sits elevated in the “bite,” offering:
    - Full view of the arena
    - The power to shout commands
    - A symbolic image of him being the force that “took the bite” from the world        
- Sand in the pit has a **reddish hue**, stained by decades of blood and copper dust    

---

### 🏟️ Arena Features

- Surrounded by **tiered stone stands** for hundreds of spectators.
- Torch sconces cast coppery and green light, flickering over the sand.
- Carved murals on arena walls depict:
    - Karzal’s past victories
    - Monsters defeated in the Pit
    - The Hollow Coin’s symbol: a battered coin with a bite taken out        

---

#### Arena Mechanics

- Trapdoors allow **beasts or fighters** to enter suddenly.
- Copper chains dangle from above, sometimes used to:
    - Restrain beasts
    - Provide dramatic swings for agile fighters
- Hidden tunnels let fighters enter from unexpected angles.    

---

## 🎟️ Audience Areas

### General Stands
- Crowded with gamblers, thugs, merchants, and citizens eager for blood sport.

### VIP Balconies
- Private alcoves with veiled curtains.
- Served by Hollow Coin couriers offering drinks, betting slips, and discreet conversations.    

---

## 💀 Karzal’s Throne
- Carved from fused **stone, copper slag, and bones** of past champions.
- Runes inlaid around the base glow faintly red.
- A **chain connects his throne to Pitlord’s Chain**, allowing him to swing it into the arena below if he chooses.    

---

### 🎲 Possible Arena Events
- Single combat challenges.
- Gladiator vs. beast matches.
- Team battles for debts or honor.
- Execution fights for traitors or rival gang spies.
- Dramatic announcements from Karzal or Zarra.    

---

## 🏰 Hollow Coin Base (Behind the Arena)
Beyond Karzal’s Pit lie the operational chambers of the Hollow Coin:

---

### 🔧 **The Armory & Smithy**
- Forges for crafting:
    - Custom weapons
    - Pit armor
    - Specialized gear for “show fights”
- Run by seasoned smiths who double as enforcers.    

---

### 💰 **The Counting House**
- Zarra Vex’s domain.
- Ledgers, coin stacks, betting slips.
- Guards posted at every corner.
- Secret vault hidden behind false copper paneling.    

---

### 🚪 **The Holding Pens**
- Cells for prisoners:
    - Fighters awaiting matches
    - Ransoms
    - Rival gang members
- Smells of sweat, blood, and mold.    

---

### 🎲 **War Room**
- Map of Tar Neôl marked with:
    - Gang territories
    - Merchant influence
    - “Debtors” and enemies
- Plans drawn up for protection rackets and retaliatory strikes.    

---

### 🛏️ **Fighter Quarters**

- Spartan sleeping spaces for loyal gladiators.    
- Walls scrawled with:
    - Names of fallen fighters
    - Tally marks of kills
    - Hollow Coin slogans like:
        “Strength fights. Weakness Dies.”

---

### 🪙 Secret Tunnels

- Web of passageways connecting:
    - The foundry
    - Arena entrances
    - Escape routes into the undercity
- Perfect for smuggling people or goods unseen.    

---

## 🎭 Atmosphere
- Smell of hot copper and blood.
- Constant metallic echo of chains, weapons, and roars.
- Flickering green and orange light from copper torches.
- A palpable sense of **discipline and danger**.    

---

## 🪙 Hooks for the Party
- Karzal may demand the party fight in the arena to prove themselves.
- Zarra might offer secret intel—for a price.
- A rival gang might attempt an assassination during a crowded arena night.
- An imprisoned contact could plead for the party’s help from the holding pens.
