## Overview

- **Name:** Father Elephas  
- **Race:** Loxodon  
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good  
- **Role:** Spiritual caretaker, healer, and ritualist of Silvermourn  
- **Inspiration:** Based on Pater Damiaan (Molokaï)  
- **Current Location:** Silvermourn, in a small hut near the old stone circle

---

## Description

Father Elephas is a towering yet humble **loxodon cleric**, draped in weathered robes sewn with patches and symbols of the many cursed souls he's helped over the years. Despite his size, he moves slowly and gently. His voice is **soft and low**, like distant thunder or a heartbeat through stone.

- Speaks slowly, deliberately, and always with compassion  
- Rarely rests — keeps vigil during every full moon  
- Refuses to own personal possessions beyond a wooden staff  
- Revered by the cursed, feared by none

---

## Personality

- **Selfless:** He lives only to serve others  
- **Gentle:** Even toward monstrous beings  
- **Wise:** Offers perspective without preaching  
- **Unyielding:** Will not abandon those in need, no matter the danger

> “There is no curse that kindness cannot bear the weight of — even if it cannot lift it.”

---

## History

- Once a renowned healer in a distant temple city  
- Left his position after becoming disillusioned with politics and luxury  
- Traveled to the ruins of **Branwen** and helped turn it into **Silvermourn**  
- Brought with him a rare **Moonlight Crystal** and created the **ritual of suppression**

---

## The Ritual

- **Focus:** A rare and massive **Moonlight Crystal**  
- **Effect:** Suppresses magical curses (lycanthropy, madness, possession, etc.)  
- **Anchor Point:** The **stone circle** rebuilt from Branwen’s old town square  
- **Current Status:** Broken — the crystal was stolen, and the cursed are losing control

---

## Connection to the Party

- May be sought out after the werewolf attack  
- **Knows Varik** and will speak of him with deep sorrow and hope  
- May plead for the recovery of the crystal or offer to guide the party spiritually  
- Will not leave Silvermourn unless every soul is safe

---

## Optional Mechanics

If needed, treat him as a **non-combatant NPC** with access to:
- **Ritual spells**: *Remove Curse*, *Calm Emotions*, *Lesser Restoration*, *Sanctuary*  
- **Special Ability**: Can empower rituals using the Moonlight Crystal (if restored)
