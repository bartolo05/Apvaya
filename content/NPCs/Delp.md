## 🧍 **Delp**

**Race:** Half-Orc  
**Role:** Palace kitchen servant / porter  
**Affiliation:** Royal Palace (service wing)  
**Status:** Ordinary witness to an anomaly  
**Disposition:** Simple, honest, cooperative

---

### Overview

Delp is a kitchen porter working in the **service wing near the royal palace kitchens**. He carries sacks, crates, and barrels between storerooms, stairwells, and courtyards. He is not particularly clever, curious, or ambitious — just reliable.

He recently experienced something that _should not have happened_, and he does not understand it.

---

### Appearance

- Broad-shouldered, thick arms, slightly hunched from carrying loads
- Wears stained work clothes and a knotted apron    
- Calloused hands, one thumb permanently bent wrong from an old break
- Keeps his hair short and practical    
- Usually smells faintly of grain dust and cooking grease

---

### Personality
- Literal and straightforward
- Answers questions honestly, without embellishment
- Uncomfortable with abstract language or theory
- Easily embarrassed if laughed at
- Not prone to panic — more confused than afraid

Delp does not seek attention. He only spoke up because **“it weren’t right.”**
