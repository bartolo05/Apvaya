_Apprentice healer of Silvermourn_

---

## Overview

**Tolgark Driftwhisker** is a young **Moruun** who traveled across distant frozen seas to study under **Father Elephas**. Though stubborn and practical by nature, Tolgark holds the old healer in deep reverence, nearly to the point of veneration.

He dreams of establishing a **quarantine sanctuary** near his homeland — a place like Silvermourn, where cursed and dangerous individuals can live safely. But he believes that such a place **cannot exist without a Moonlight Crystal**.

> “A sanctuary without a crystal is just a camp. The magic is what holds the peace.”  
> — Tolgark

Father Elephas loves him like a son, but quietly knows: **Tolgark is not yet ready**. His heart is noble, but his mind clings to practical solutions over spiritual truth.

## Physical Description

- **Race:** Moruun (walrus-folk)
- **Height:** 4’10”
- **Build:** Broad and muscular, covered in travel-worn wraps
- **Skin:** Salt-dark gray with patches of barnacle-scars on his back
- **Tusk Jewelry:** Wears copper rings engraved with protective runes
- **Eyes:** Wide, brown, and full of certainty
- **Voice:** Slow but passionate — especially when defending his beliefs

---

## Personality

- **Alignment:** Lawful Good    
- **Traits:** Loyal, focused, driven, slightly impatient
- **Beliefs:** Safety comes from magic and control — not just kindness
- **Habits:**
    - Keeps a **leather-bound journal** of his learnings from Elephas
    - Stirs tea or potions **counterclockwise** for good luck
    - Hums ancestral lullabies while treating wounds

---

## Backstory

When Tolgark was young, a **contagious magical affliction** known as the **Red Sleep** (or **Bloodcurse**) swept through his coastal village in the northern isles. It began with a fevered madness and ended in death. The illness arrived on the corpse of a sea-witch washed ashore.

His older sibling, **Turell**, was the first to fall ill. Despite Tolgark's efforts — with herbs, chants, even borrowed rituals — nothing worked. When more villagers began to show symptoms, the elders **cast the afflicted adrift on an ice-raft**.

Tolgark watched helplessly as Turell disappeared into the mist.

He swore no one would ever be abandoned like that again. He believes that if a place like **Silvermourn** had existed near his homeland, **Turell could have been saved**.

Now he studies **anti-curse rituals and healing magic**, convinced that the **Moonlight Crystal** is the key to saving others. His obsession is driven by guilt and helplessness. Father Elephas sees this and knows Tolgark still has a lesson to learn:

> “Love wasn’t enough to save Turell. So I will find what is.”

## Goals

- Establish a **new sanctuary colony** near his icy homeland    
- Obtain a **Moonlight Crystal** to anchor its ritual of protection
- Master **healing and curse suppression magic**
- Make Father Elephas proud

---

## Relationships

### Father Elephas
Tolgark sees him as the perfect example of wisdom, power, and purpose.  
He will **follow him into danger**, carry him if needed, and fight anyone who disrespects him.

> “When I build the sanctuary, he will come with me. He has to.”  
> — Tolgark, softly

---

## Arc Potential

- May clash with others who question the importance of the Moonlight Crystal    
- Could endanger himself or others by trying to retrieve one too soon
- His growth lies in learning that **community is greater than the tools used to create it**
