## 🪙 Zarra “The Ledger” Vex

**Race:** Female Hobgoblin  
**Age:** Late 30s  
**Faction:** Hollow Coin Syndicate  
**Role:** Second-in-Command (Treasurer & Betting Master)  
**Location:** Karzal’s Pit, The Brazen Gritpour

---

### 🧱 Appearance

- Lean, wiry build.
- Coppery-red skin, angular features.
- Black hair pulled into a tight braid.
- Tattoos resembling rows of **numbers and tally marks** running down her arms.
- Wears a **long red coat** with hidden pockets.
- Always carries a small black ledger and copper stylus.

---

### 🧠 Personality

- Precise, sharp-tongued, pragmatic.    
- Highly organized; memory like a steel trap.
- Speaks bluntly and wastes no words.
- Fiercely loyal to Karzal—but knows when to cover herself.
- Deadly calm, even under threat.

---

### 🪙 Skills & Role

- Runs all betting during arena fights.    
- Tracks:
    - Syndicate finances
    - Rackets and debts
    - Who owes favors to whom
- Keeps secret ledgers with blackmail material as insurance.
- Good with:
    - Daggers
    - Throwing knives
    - Blinding copper dust

---

### 🎭 Dialogue Snippets

> “Names. Faces. Debts. That’s my world. So which are you here to settle?”
> “Odds on the wyvern stand at fifteen to one. Care to tempt fate?”
> “Careful. Even Karzal’s muscle can’t break numbers once they’re inked in my book.”

---

### Hooks

- Knows who bet on the wyvern—and who profited from the sword’s disappearance.    
- May sell information—for a price.
- Possibly keeps a secret ledger with dirt on Karzal.

---

**Vibe:** Cold, calculating, utterly professional—a woman whose **numbers are sharper than any blade.**
