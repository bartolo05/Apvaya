*Sturdy coastal folk of tusks, tide, and tradition.*

---

## Overview

The **Moruun** are walrus-like humanoids who dwell where **ancient forests meet icy coasts**. Shorter than humans but stockier than dwarves, they are defined by their **resilience**, **loyalty**, and a deep cultural tie to the **sea and stone**.

Though slow to speak and act, Moruun are **unyielding in principle** and often revered as keepers of balance between nature and community.

---

## Physical Traits

- **Height:** 4’6” to 5’0”  
- **Build:** Stocky, broad-shouldered, and tusked  
- **Skin:** Leathery gray, brown, or dark taupe  
- **Distinguishing Features:** Long tusks, wrinkled hide, thick whiskers  
- **Natural Adaptations:** Cold-resistant, strong swimmers

---

## Personality

- **Stubborn but kind** — once their mind is made up, it rarely changes  
- **Loyal to their communities**, often forming life-long bonds of service  
- **Patient and deliberate**, especially in speech and decision-making  
- Prefer practicality over pride, and tradition over ambition

---

## Culture & Society

- **Settlements:** Small villages near coasts and forest edges  
- **Architecture:** Driftwood huts, whale bone beams, kelp-rope moorings  
- **Crafting:** Use of **wood, stone, and underwater salvage** (coral, shell, shipwrecks)  
- **Magic:** Mostly ritualistic or spiritual — some are **moon-callers** or **tide-wardens**  
- **Tools:** Spears, anchors, fishhooks, stone hammers, and netted slings

---

## Common Roles

- **Healers**, **fishers**, **ritualists**, **tide-guardians**, and **builders**  
- Occasionally serve as **assistants to other spiritual leaders**, such as Father Elephas

---

## Naming Conventions

- First names often have **rolling syllables**: *Ukkuna*, *Boruun*, *Tommek*, *Saalu*  
- Family names are descriptive: *Stonewake*, *Driftwhisker*, *Kelpward*

---

## Racial Traits (5e-Compatible)

- **Ability Score Increase:** +2 Constitution, +1 Wisdom  
- **Size:** Medium  
- **Speed:** 25 ft.  
- **Swim Speed:** 20 ft.  
- **Cold Adaptation:** Resistance to cold damage  
- **Thick Hide:** Unarmored AC = 13 + Constitution modifier  
- **Tusks:** 1d6 piercing damage (Strength-based natural weapon)  
- **Steady Soul:** Advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened  
- **Tool Proficiency:** Choice of woodcarver’s tools, fisherman’s tools, or herbalism kit  
- **Skill Proficiency:** Insight or Medicine (your choice)  
- **Languages:** Common, Moruun (guttural, vowel-heavy, often sung)
