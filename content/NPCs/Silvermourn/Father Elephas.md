## Overview

- **Name:** Father Elephas  
- **Race:** Loxodon  
- **Alignment:** Lawful Good  
- **Role:** Spiritual caretaker, healer, and ritualist of Silvermourn  
- **Current Location:** Silvermourn

---

## Description

Father Elephas is a towering yet humble **loxodon cleric**, draped in weathered robes sewn with patches and symbols of the many cursed souls he's helped over the years. Despite his size, he moves slowly and gently. His voice is **soft and low**, like distant thunder or a heartbeat through stone.

- Speaks slowly, deliberately, and always with compassion  
- Rarely rests — keeps vigil during every full moon  
- Revered by the cursed, feared by none

---

## Personality

- **Selfless:** He lives only to serve others  
- **Gentle:** Even toward monstrous beings  
- **Wise:** Offers perspective without preaching  
- **Unyielding:** Will not abandon those in need, no matter the danger

> “There is no curse that kindness cannot bear the weight of — even if it cannot lift it.”
