## 💀 Karzal “The Pit Butcher” Dravak

**Race:** Goliath (Half-Ogre heritage hints)  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** Late 50s – early 60s  
**Faction:** Hollow Coin Syndicate (Boss)  
**Location:** Karzal’s Pit, beneath The Brazen Gritpour

---
### 🧱 Appearance

- Towering frame, over 7 feet tall, built like a mountain of rock and muscle.
- Slate-gray skin streaked with veins of metallic iron dust and hints of green verdigris.
- Bald, thick brow ridge, square jaw, and a broad, flattened nose.
- One eye replaced by a **coin-sized obsidian disc**, a mark of an old infernal bargain or punishment.
- Covered in ritual scars and old battle wounds.   
- Wears a **red-plated pauldron** and carries a massive spiked flail.    

---
### 🩸 Personality
- Gruff, commanding presence; speaks few words but each carries weight.    
- Values strength, loyalty, and **visible dominance** above all else.    
- Sees **information and subtlety as “coward’s tools.”**    
- Fiercely protective of his crew—those who earn his loyalty have it for life.    
- Deep distrust and hatred of shapeshifters and illusions.    
- Superstitious fear of magic he can’t see or smash.    

---
### 🕳️ Background

- Born in the undercity slums, originally fought in illegal pit fights for coin.    
- Rose to prominence after **slaying three rival gang leaders single-handedly** during an arena riot.    
- Earned his nickname _“Pit Butcher”_ for both his fighting style and ruthlessness.    
- Became enforcer for the Hollow Coin, then took over after previous boss vanished under suspicious circumstances.    
- Rumors hint he once **made a pact** for power or survived a curse, resulting in his obsidian eye.    

---

### 🛡️ Role in Tar Neôl

- Runs the Hollow Coin as:    
    - **Muscle-for-hire** for other crime lords, merchants, and even city officials.
    - Controllers of underground **pit fights** and blood sport betting.        
    - **Enforcers** collecting debts, crushing rebellions, and intimidating rival factions.        
- Keeps the Hollow Coin extremely **organized and disciplined**, despite their brutish reputation.    
- Known for running **Karzal’s Pit**, a gladiatorial arena beneath the Brazen Gritpour.    

---

### 🪙 The Hollow Coin’s Reputation

- Feared for **raw physical dominance** and swift, brutal retaliation.    
- Rarely subtle, but their sheer numbers and loyalty make them nearly impossible to uproot.    
- Strong connections in the industrial sector, controlling security for copper transport and foundries.    
- Karzal keeps his fighters loyal by paying well and rewarding strength.    

---

### 🔥 Unique Item: Pitlord’s Chain

- Massive spiked flail called **Pitlord’s Chain**.    
- When slammed into the ground:    
    - Shakes the arena floor in a 10-ft radius.        
    - DC 15 STR save or knocked prone.        
- Said to whisper in Karzal’s mind, urging violence.    

---

### 🎭 Relations to Current Plot

- Furious over the theft of **Cindervow** (Flametongue sword).    
- Believes the Jelly Boys are responsible due to Ghaldran’s deception.    
- Prepares to declare open war if the sword is not returned.    
- May test or challenge the party in **Karzal’s Pit** before trusting them.    
- Potentially willing to strike a deal if the party proves strong enough.    

---

### Potential Scenes

- Karzal seated on his stone throne, flail across his knees, staring down at the party from above the pit.    
- Barking orders mid-fight, tossing weaker fighters into the arena as fodder.    
- Quietly sharing a drink with a trusted few, reflecting on how the undercity has changed since his youth.    

---

### Plot Hooks

- Secretly worried about shapeshifters undermining his faction’s strength.    
- Might hire the party to hunt down “Needle” (Ghaldran) once the truth emerges.    
- Sees the party as potential assets—or threats—to Hollow Coin dominance.    

---

## 🌆 Vibe

Karzal is a **living monument to brute power** who’s grown older and smarter—but no less dangerous. The undercity respects him because he’s endured decades in the pits and refuses to bow to anyone.
