_To the associates of Professor Vael Torith,_

Professor Torith has informed me that he is prepared to begin the preliminary work required for the restoration of your companion's damaged extradimensional refuge.

However, as discussed during your recent visit to the Collegium Arcanum, such an undertaking lies at the very limits of contemporary planar scholarship. Before the Professor can safely proceed, he requires access to a particular work presently housed within the Royal Archives of the Palace of Tar Neôl.

The volume in question is entitled:

**_The Atlas Intermundia: A Treatise on Planar Junctions and Extradimensional Spaces_**

Though much of the text is historical in nature, it contains several observations regarding dimensional cohesion, resonance, and the long-term stability of enclosed spaces—subjects directly relevant to the restoration of damaged demiplanes.

Unfortunately, the Royal Archivist does not permit this volume to leave the Archives under any circumstances. Repeated scholarly petitions have thus far been declined.

Ordinarily, Professor Torith would pursue the matter personally. Regrettably, his ongoing research into planar disturbances within the region demands his near-constant presence within his observatory. The apparatus he maintains cannot safely be left unattended for extended periods, and the Professor rarely leaves the tower for more than a few hours at a time.

As such, he humbly requests your assistance in acquiring the volume and delivering it to his private study within the Collegium.

The Atlas is considered one of the oldest surviving works on planar theory in existence. The manuscript is therefore exceedingly fragile. Should you undertake this task, Professor Torith insists that the book be handled with the utmost care and returned in precisely the condition in which it was found.

Upon receipt of the volume, the Professor shall begin the necessary preparations for the restoration ritual without delay.

Should you accept this request, please present yourselves at the Collegium at your earliest convenience.

With respect,

**Lethia**  
Archivist and Research Assistant to Professor Vael Torith  
Department of Planar Studies  
Collegium Arcanum
