# 🎭 **Showstopper’s Prism**

_Wondrous Item (rare), requires attunement_

A **multifaceted crystal prism**, each face cut at a subtly impossible angle.  
It catches even the faintest glimmer of light and breaks it into **cascadingx ribbons of shifting color**.  
Hold it up, and each facet reflects a different scene — applause, stage lights, silhouettes, flames, and illusions of forgotten performances.

Even in darkness, it faintly glows with **spotlight hues**: gold, violet, turquoise, rose.

Anyone wearing it becomes _just a little more noticeable_ — not magically, but theatrically.

---

## ✨ **Showstopper Strike (1/day)**

When you hit with a weapon attack or cast a spell that deals damage, you may activate the prism.

When you do:

### ⭐ **Deal maximum possible damage instead of rolling.**

- Every die is treated as rolling its highest value.
- A blast of dazzling prismatic light erupts around you, harmless but spectacular.

### ⭐ **Inspire the Crowd**

Within 1 hour after using this feature, you have **advantage on one Charisma (Performance or Persuasion) check.**

This can be:

- impressing nobles,
- calming a panicked crowd,
- negotiating with circus representatives,
- dazzling a tavern full of patrons,
- or delivering a speech or prayer.

---

## 🎨 **Flavor Effects (non-mechanical)**

Whenever the prism is activated or catches strong light:
- A ghostly echo of applause can sometimes be heard.
- Minor illusory stage lights flicker across the user’s shoulders.
- Their shadow briefly splits into several synchronized silhouettes.
- The prism’s facets briefly show scenes of past circus victories or performances.

NPCs absolutely **will** comment on it.
