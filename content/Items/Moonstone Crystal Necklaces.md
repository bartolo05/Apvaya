## Soft-Suppression Field
- The Wearer is protected against minor curses
- Effects of Major curses are lessened
- Spells the wearer casts cost one spell slot higher
- Cantrips cast by the wearer do only half damage.
## Clarity Pulses (1/day)
Ends the Frightened, Charmed, or Confused condition, Lasts 1 minute

The crystal emits a soft silver glow
