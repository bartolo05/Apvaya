### Beschrijving
Een handelspand van goede kwaliteit in de betere wijken van Tar Neôl.
Het Ecliptisch Emporium verkoopt alchemische ingrediënten, natuurlijke producten met magische effecten, ornamentele schedels en lichaamsdelen van exotische monsters en 


#### Alchemical Ingredients
- **Fireflower Petals** – Used to create potions of fire resistance or alchemical fire.
- **Voidglass Shards** – Light-absorbing crystal dust, useful in illusion magic.
- **Moonshadow Ink** – Ink that glows under moonlight; used for hidden messages.
- **Ironbark Shavings** – Enhances potion durability and armor reinforcement.
- **Emberdust** – A glowing powder that can be used in explosive alchemy.

#### Ritual Components
- **Ectoplasm Vials** – Harvested from spirits; required for necromantic rituals.
- **Crushed Star Coral** – Enhances water-based magic and divination spells.
- **Phoenix Ash** – Mixed with healing potions to boost regeneration effects.
- **Rune-etched Bones** – Used in summoning rituals and protective wards.
- **Blood Quartz** – Strengthens blood magic and sacrificial rites.

#### Potions & Mixtures
- **Alchemist’s Solvent** – Dissolves non-magical materials (good for breaking locks).
- **Dustbreather Extract** – Grants short-term immunity to inhaled poisons.
- **Tonic of the Restless Mind** – Helps resist mind control effects.
- **Shifting Dye** – A liquid dye that changes colors with heat or magic.
- **Serpent’s Reflex Potion** – Boosts reaction speed for a short duration.

### Illicit & Hidden Goods (Undercity Operations)
These items are not openly sold but can be acquired through Zhiran’s connections.

#### Mutagenic & Dangerous Substances
- **Essence of Duskbloom** – Enhances magic but drains life force; highly addictive.
- **Graveblossom Nectar** – Induces visions of the afterlife (used by death cults).
- **Vial of Hollowing** – Drains emotions and weakens the soul; rumored to be a tool of assassins.
- **Duskrin Blood-Sap** – A narcotic that enhances darkvision but makes light painful.
- **Oozeheart Extract** – Causes slow mutations when injected over time.

#### Illegal Artifacts & Materials
- **Shards of the Unspoken Pact** – Forbidden contract fragments that bind souls.
- **Caged Wisp** – A living wisp spirit used as a power source or interrogated for lost knowledge.
- **Gilded Bone Dice** – Used by high-stakes gamblers, allegedly rigged with magic.
- **Tome of Forgotten Oaths** – A book containing lost, cursed rituals.
- **Abyssal Chitin Slivers** – Bits of carapace from an unknown deep-dwelling creature.

#### Contraband & Smuggled Goods
- **Darksteel Weapons** – Blades infused with void energy, illegal in some cities.
- **Whispering Coins** – Enchanted coins that carry coded messages.
- **Alchemy-Forged Disguises** – Temporary shapeshifting masks.
- **Sable Lotus Oil** – Deadly toxin that kills without visible traces.
- **Marked Playing Cards** – Used for high-stakes cheating.
