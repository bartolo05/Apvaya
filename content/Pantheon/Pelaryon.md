## 🌊 Pelaryon — _The Endless Tide_

Pelaryon governs **movement that cannot be stopped**. The sea connects continents, cultures, and eras. It is the god of:

- Journeys that change people
- Things carried far from their origin
- The inevitability of motion

Pelaryon is not freedom in the abstract — he is **movement regardless of desire**.

**Worshippers**

- Sailors, explorers, messengers, migrants
- Those who leave home knowing they may never return

**Mythic truth**

> _Nothing placed in the sea remains where it was._
