## 🧱 Caelreth — _The Bound Horizon_

Caelreth represents the **end of travel**.

Not death — but the moment when nothing can go further.

Where Pelaryon erodes borders, Caelreth **creates them**.

**Domains**

- Walls, borders, thresholds, fortifications
- Stagnation, isolation, containment
- Finality of decisions
- “This far, and no further”

**Conceptual opposition**
- Pelaryon connects → Caelreth separates
- Pelaryon erodes → Caelreth preserves
- Pelaryon carries → Caelreth anchors

**Worshippers**

- City-builders, wardens, jailers, judges
- Those who fear change more than cruelty
- Cultures that survived invasion by _refusing to move_



**Uncomfortable truth**
> Caelreth prevents collapse — but also prevents escape
