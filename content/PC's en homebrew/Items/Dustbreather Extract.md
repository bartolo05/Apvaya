_Wondrous Item, rare, 300GP_

*A thick, dusky-colored liquid stored in a small, airtight vial. When uncorked, the extract releases a faint, shimmering dust that seems to move unnaturally in the air. The scent is dry and metallic, like ancient stone crumbling to dust.*


![](img/Dustbreather%20extract.jpg)

### **Effects:**
- When consumed, **Dustbreather Extract** allows the user to survive without air for **1 hour**, making them immune to suffocation, inhaled toxins, and gas-based spells or effects like _Cloudkill_ or _Stinking Cloud_.
- If applied externally to the skin (requires an action), the user gains **resistance to non-magical bludgeoning damage** for 10 minutes, as their body partially takes on the properties of compacted dust and stone.
