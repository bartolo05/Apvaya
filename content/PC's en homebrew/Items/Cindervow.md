# ⚔️ The Cindervow

Glowing with heat that never fades, this sword carries the will of **Fyntheros**.

When inactive, the Cindervow appears as a simple **blackened metal handle**, warm to the touch but bearing **no blade**. In this dormant form, it serves merely as a hilt — silent and inert.

When the **command word** is spoken, however, a blade of radiant fire and molten light ignites from the handle, shaping itself into **any sword-like weapon** the wielder desires — a longsword, rapier, scimitar, or even a greatsword.

The Cindervow is a **+2 magical weapon**.  
When you hit with it, the target takes an **additional 1d6 fire damage**.

Its form may shift, but its flame never wavers — a fragment of Fyntheros’s eternal vow made manifest.
