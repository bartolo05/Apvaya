# 🔍 The Seer’s Lens

This finely crafted **magnifying glass** hums with faint arcane resonance. Its rim is engraved with shifting glyphs that catch the light at strange angles — each symbol flickering like an eye that blinks open and shut.

When held before the eye, the lens reveals faint traces of **magic** shimmering through the air, and the blurred outlines of **invisible creatures or objects** lurking nearby.  
Those who gaze through it for too long, however, claim to see things beyond mortal comprehension — echoes of the Weave itself, staring back.

---

### Properties

- **Type:** Wondrous Item
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Requires Attunement:** No
- **Effect:**
  - While looking through the lens, you can cast **Detect Magic** at will, without expending a spell slot.
  - You can also use it to **see invisible creatures and objects** as if under the effect of the _See Invisibility_ spell.
  - The lens must be held up to your eye for the duration of these effects.
