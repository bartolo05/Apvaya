_Wand, rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)_

This warped-looking wand is warm to the touch and flickers faintly with unstable flames. It smells faintly of singed hair and unpredictability.

- While holding this wand, you gain a **+1 bonus to spell attack rolls**.
- The wand allows you to cast a unique version of **Fire Bolt**, known as **Wonky Firebolt**, as an action.

#### **Wonky Firebolt**

_Evocation Cantrip_

**Casting Time:** 1 action  
**Range:** 120 feet  
**Components:** V, S  
**Duration:** Instantaneous

You hurl a wobbling mote of chaotic fire at a creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. Roll a d20 after declaring your target:

- **1:** The spell backfires spectacularly—the firebolt is redirected at **you**, the caster. Roll the attack against your own AC.
- **2–18:** The spell behaves normally. The firebolt hits the target if the attack roll succeeds, dealing damage as per Fire Bolt:
  - 1d10 fire damage (increases with level: 2d10 at 5th, 3d10 at 11th, 4d10 at 17th).
- **19–20:** A surge of volatile magic erupts from the wand. Instead of a firebolt, **Fireball** is cast centered on the original target (DC 15 Dex save).
  - The fireball deals **8d6 fire damage** on a failed save, or half as much on a success.

This chaotic magic cannot be controlled, and **you cannot choose to trigger the Fireball intentionally**—it always results from a 19 or 20 on the wonky fire check, nor can you choose not to trigger the fireball if you roll a 19 or 20.
